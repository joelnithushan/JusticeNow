## How we contribute

1. Branch from `main` (`feat/…`, `fix/…`, `test/…`, `docs/…`, `chore/…`).
2. Commit under **your own** `user.name` / `user.email` — never someone else’s identity.
3. Open a PR into `main`. **Do not merge your own PR.**
4. Follow [`CLAUDE.md`](./CLAUDE.md) anonymity and architecture rules.
5. Prefer several small, reviewable commits over one opaque dump.

See also [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for the full workflow.
